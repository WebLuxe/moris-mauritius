By region

Four coasts, four moods

Where you stay shapes your trip. Here's how each side of the island feels.

North

Lively & sunny

The most developed coast, with calm beaches, plenty of restaurants and easy access to Port Louis and the northern islets.

  • Grand Baie for food and nightlife
  • Boat trips to the northern islets
  • Pamplemousses Botanical Garden
West

Sunsets & mountains

The dry, sheltered west coast faces the sunset, with long beaches, Tamarin Bay and the mountains of Black River behind.

  • Flic en Flac and Tamarin beaches
  • Dolphin-watching boat trips
  • Le Morne for the classic view
South

Wild & green

The least developed side of the island, with dramatic cliffs, sugar cane fields, waterfalls and winding coastal roads.

  • Gris Gris cliffs and the Maconde viewpoint
  • Chamarel and Black River Gorges
  • Quiet villages and roadside food stalls
East

Breezy & turquoise

The trade winds keep the east cooler, and its lagoons are some of the widest and bluest on the island.

  • Île aux Cerfs by boat
  • Belle Mare's long beaches
  • Snorkelling in Blue Bay Marine Park
